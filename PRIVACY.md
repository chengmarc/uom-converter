# Privacy Policy

**UOM Converter**
Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy covers UOM Converter in every form it ships in: the Microsoft Store edition, the
Windows installer, and the portable exe.

## Summary

UOM Converter does not collect, store, send, or share any personal information. It works entirely
on your computer and never connects to the internet.

## What the app does with data

- **No network access.** UOM Converter has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, advertising,
  accounts, or update checks. It never sends data to the developer or to anyone else.
- **Settings stay on your computer.** The app remembers the role and region you picked, and the
  window's size and position, in its data folder on your computer:
  - Microsoft Store edition: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\<package>\` (removed when you uninstall the app)
  - Installer and portable exe: `%APPDATA%\UOM Converter\`
- **Your numbers aren't saved.** Values you type are used to work out the answer on screen and are
  not stored or sent anywhere. When you click a result, it is copied to your clipboard and nothing
  else.

## Third parties

UOM Converter does not include any third-party services that collect data. Microsoft may collect
information about Store downloads and installs under the
[Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). That collection is
separate from UOM Converter.

## Children

UOM Converter does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
