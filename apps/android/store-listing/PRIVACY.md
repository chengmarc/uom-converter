# Privacy Policy

**UOM Converter for Android**\
Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy covers the UOM Converter Android app on Google Play.

## Summary

UOM Converter does not collect, store, send, or share any personal information. The app works
entirely on your device and never connects to the internet.

## What the app does

- **No network access.** It has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, accounts, or
  update checks, and it loads nothing but its own files. It never sends data to the developer or
  to anyone else.
- **Settings stay on your phone.** The app remembers the role, region and language you picked and
  the converters you opened last, in its own storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or
  clearing its storage in Android's settings, removes them.
- **Your numbers aren't saved.** Values you type are used to work out the answer on screen and are
  not stored or sent anywhere. When you tap a result, it is copied to your clipboard and nothing
  else.

## Third parties

UOM Converter does not include any third-party services that collect data. Google may collect
information about Play downloads and installs under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). That collection is separate from UOM
Converter.

## Children

UOM Converter does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
