UOM Converter Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy covers UOM Converter for Windows, from the Microsoft Store or the installer on GitHub, and the website at https://uom-converter.com/.

Summary

UOM Converter does not collect, store, send or share any personal information. The Windows app works entirely on your computer and never connects to the internet. The website does the same once loaded: it works out every answer in your browser and sends nothing back.

What the App Does With Data

• No network access. UOM Converter has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, accounts or update checks. It never sends data to the developer or to anyone else.
• Your numbers aren't saved. Values you type are used to work out the answer on screen and are not stored or sent anywhere. When you click a result, it is copied to your clipboard and nothing else.
• Settings stay on your computer. The app remembers the role and region you picked, and the window's size and position, in its data folder: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\<package>\ for the Microsoft Store edition, removed when you uninstall the app, or %APPDATA%\UOM Converter\ for the installer from GitHub.

The Website

• Same as the app. The website runs the same converters as the Windows app. It has no analytics, cookies, advertising or accounts, and loads nothing from other sites.
• Settings stay in your browser. It remembers the role and region you picked in your browser's storage for the site. Clearing the site's data removes them.
• Offline copy. Your browser may keep a copy of the site's pages and files so it works without a connection. Nothing you type is part of that copy.
• Hosting. The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web server, Cloudflare receives your IP address and the pages you request, under its own privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/. The site does not use Cloudflare's analytics or add any of its scripts to the page.

Microsoft may collect information about Store downloads and installs under the Microsoft Privacy Statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). That collection is separate from UOM Converter.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here and the "Last updated" date will change.
